# Business Data Analysis Using Tableau – Box Plot Visualization

## Project Overview

This project presents a business data analysis dashboard created using Tableau Public. The Customer Shopping Dataset was analyzed to understand product pricing, total sales, customer age distribution, payment preferences, and purchasing patterns.

A Box Plot is included to examine the distribution of product prices across different categories.

## Dataset

* Dataset: Customer Shopping Dataset
* Source: Kaggle
* Records: 99,457 transactions
* Tool Used: Tableau Public

## Dataset Fields

* Customer ID
* Gender
* Age
* Category
* Quantity
* Price
* Payment Method
* Invoice Number
* Invoice Date
* Shopping Mall

## Calculated Field

Total Sales was calculated using the following formula:

```text
[Price] * [Quantity]
```

## Dashboard Visualizations

1. Box Plot – Price Distribution by Product Category
2. Bar Chart – Total Sales by Product Category
3. Pie Chart – Preferred Payment Method
4. Histogram – Customer Age Distribution
5. Scatter Plot – Price vs Quantity by Product Category

## Business Insights

1. Clothing generates the highest total sales of approximately 113.99 million.
2. Shoes and Technology are the second and third highest revenue-generating product categories.
3. Technology has the widest and highest price distribution among all product categories.
4. Cash is the most preferred payment method with 44,447 transactions.
5. Customer purchases are distributed across a wide age range, indicating demand from multiple age groups.

## Business Recommendations

1. The business should prioritize Clothing, Shoes, and Technology because they generate the highest sales.
2. Premium marketing campaigns can be created for Technology products due to their higher price range.
3. Cash and credit-card payment processes should be optimized because they are the most frequently used payment methods.
